Privacy

How we handle information from this website and our staff app.

This page explains what the Midwest website collects through estimate requests and page-view reporting, what the Midwest Operations staff app sends to our server, and how the office team uses it.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Information you submit

When you fill out the estimate form, Midwest receives the contact and project information you provide, including your name, phone number, email address, lake, property address, timing, and project details.

How that information is used

Midwest uses estimate-request information to review your project, contact you about service, and coordinate sales, repair, installation, removal, storage, winterization, or shoreline support.

Basic page reporting

The website also sends limited page-view information to the Midwest admin app, including page path, page title, referrer, and a session identifier. This helps the company understand which pages are being used most often and where visitors are finding the site.

Midwest Operations staff app

Midwest Equipment Dock and Lift Services, LLC publishes a mobile app called Midwest Operations. It is an internal tool for our own office and field staff and requires a Midwest staff account to sign in. It is not intended for customers or the general public, and there is no public sign-up.

What the staff app sends to us

When a staff member uses the app, it sends information to Midwest's own server at app.midwestdockandlift.com:

  • The email address and password used to sign in. The sign-in session token is stored only on the staff member's own device.
  • Customer and property information the team views or edits, including customer name, phone numbers, email address, service address, lake, equipment details, and service notes.
  • Work records created in the field, including job and payment status, field checklists, service notes, and messages between staff and dispatch.
  • Photos taken with the device camera or chosen from the device photo library and attached to a customer, equipment, or marketing record.
  • The staff account identifier attached to those records, so we know who did the work. Our server also keeps a sign-in log that includes the account, the email used, the time, the IP address, and the device's app identification string, for account security.

All of this is used only to run the business: scheduling, routing, service history, invoicing, customer support, and account security. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to track staff or customers across other companies' apps or websites.

Location in the staff app

The staff app asks for permission to use the device's location while the app is in use. That location is used on the device only. It works out which assigned customer stop is closest to the staff member and hands a destination address to the phone's maps app for directions. The app does not send the staff member's location to Midwest, does not record it, and does not track staff movement or work history by location. Declining the location permission only turns off the nearest-client shortcut; the rest of the app works normally.

Third parties in the staff app

The staff app contains no advertising, attribution, crash-reporting, or third-party analytics software. Maps in the app are drawn with the operating system's own mapping component. When a staff member taps for directions, the phone's maps application opens with the destination address, and that handoff is governed by the maps provider's own privacy policy. When a staff member uses the app to publish a post to the company's Facebook page, the post text and any image attached are sent to Facebook (Meta) to be published on the company's public page.

Customer information in the staff app

Some information in the app is about Midwest customers rather than about the staff member using it. Customers can ask what we hold about them, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@midwestdockandlift.com or call the office, and we will respond within 30 days. We may need to keep some records to meet tax, warranty, or other legal obligations, and we will say so if that applies to your request.

Staff accounts and deletion requests

Staff accounts are created and removed by the Midwest office, not through the app. A staff member who wants their account and its sign-in history removed can ask the office, and accounts are deactivated when a staff member leaves. Requests about any information held in the app, from staff or customers, go to info@midwestdockandlift.com.

How long information is kept

Customer and job records, including photos and notes, are kept while the customer relationship is active and afterwards for as long as our tax, warranty, and legal obligations require. Sign-in and security logs are kept for account security and cleared periodically. Estimate requests are kept as part of the customer record. We delete information on request where we are not required to keep it.

No sale of personal data

Midwest does not sell personal information collected through this website or through the Midwest Operations staff app. Information submitted through the site is used to respond to sales and service inquiries and to improve the website experience.
